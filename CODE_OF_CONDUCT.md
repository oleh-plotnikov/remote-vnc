# Code of Conduct

This is a small project. The rules are short because they can be.

## What is expected

Be decent to the people you are talking to. Assume the person on the other side
is acting in good faith, is working with information you may not have, and has
their own constraints on time and attention.

Criticise code, decisions and ideas as sharply as you like — that is what review
is for. Do not criticise people. There is a real difference between "this drops
the token check" and "you are careless", and only one of them helps.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Comments demeaning someone for who they are — nationality, gender, sexuality,
  disability, appearance, religion, age, level of experience
- Sexual attention or imagery
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Reporting

If someone behaves this way, tell the maintainer privately: message
[@oleh-plotnikov](https://github.com/oleh-plotnikov) on GitHub. Reports are
handled confidentially, and you will not be asked to justify being uncomfortable.

For behaviour that breaks GitHub's own rules, you can also report it directly
through [GitHub](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse), which can act in ways
a repository maintainer cannot.

## Enforcement

Depending on what happened, the response ranges from a private word to editing or
removing comments, closing a thread, or blocking someone from the repository.
Repeated or severe behaviour goes straight to a block.

This applies anywhere the project is being discussed: issues, pull requests,
commit messages, and reviews.
